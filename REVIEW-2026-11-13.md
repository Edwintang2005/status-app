Move them onto `RunGate`; `isBusy` → `pairingOperation: PairingOperation?`; one `RetryPacing` for throttle, storage-full, status backoff and rejected uploads.

3. **Archive as a stream (redesign A, APP-1's rest).** Chunks of ~25, batch-fetched with `desiredKeys` = the one asset straight into staging, failures classified (transient / gone / unsupported), `gone` listed for explicit confirmation.

5. **Duplicate UI (UI-7).** Four report dialogs, two identical caption fields, two Save-to-Photos routines, three hand-styled capsules.
