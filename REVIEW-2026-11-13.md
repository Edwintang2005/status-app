## Wasted cloud calls and work

What a cold launch costs today: 2–3 round trips, 4–5 with the APP-3 race. A push: 1 fetch + 1 media. A send: 1.

- **STORE-5 step 3** — let `mutate` derive widget reloads from a "what widgets draw" projection and delete the `reloadWidgets:` parameter (36 call sites decide by hand). Guard with a test walking `WidgetContent`'s inputs.
- **STORE-6 step 3** — the index is one 1.1 MB blob rewritten whole per moment; split hot (~200 newest, what extensions need) from cold.
- **APP-11** — `send()` still rewrites the index and snapshot and prunes media on the main actor as the composer dismisses; `CrashReporter` parses JSON synchronously at launch; audio session activation on main.
- **CLOUD-10** — a status change is two sequential saves (three on a stale cache). One non-atomic `modifyRecords` for status + log.
- **CLOUD-11 / APP-12** — captions have no rejected backoff; missing favourites retry forever (now bounded, but still every pass); receipts marked abandoned even offline; the owner's share check runs every cold launch and hourly after the link is closed; the account cache expires every 5 min in the app; a Siri heart triggers a full refresh; archive and recap fetch one record per moment (batch them).

## Overcomplicated — redesign candidates

1. **Re-entrancy gates (CLOUD-12, APP-9, rest).** `RunGate` now runs the sync and recovery passes; `Outbox`'s nine `isRetrying…`/`isRepublishing…` flags and `AppModel`'s `isBusy` still hand-roll it. Move them onto `RunGate`; `isBusy` → `pairingOperation: PairingOperation?`; one `RetryPacing` for throttle, storage-full, status backoff and rejected uploads.

3. **Batched app resync (CLOUD-3)** — above; build it in `SyncCoordinator`.
4. **Archive as a stream (redesign A, APP-1's rest).** Chunks of ~25, batch-fetched with `desiredKeys` = the one asset straight into staging, failures classified (transient / gone / unsupported), `gone` listed for explicit confirmation.
5. **View decisions out of views (UI-6).** `HomeView.activeNotice`, `SyncFooter`'s precedence, `SettingsView.saveMemories/end` (the destructive gate), gallery `settle`, the status draft rules, `consumePendingRoute` → pure types in `Shared` with table tests.
6. **Duplicate UI (UI-7).** Four report dialogs, two identical caption fields, two Save-to-Photos routines, three hand-styled capsules.
