## Wasted cloud calls and work

What a cold launch costs today: 2–3 round trips, 4–5 with the APP-3 race. A push: 1 fetch + 1 media. A send: 1.

- **Archive and recap batching (CLOUD-11's rest)** — they still fetch one record per moment; batching belongs to redesign A (chunks with their own deadline), not under the current per-item bound, where one slow photo would hold a whole batch.
- **Audio session (APP-11's rest)** — `VoicePlayer`/`VoiceRecorder` activate the session on the main thread; moving it off makes play and record asynchronous through the memo views.
- **CLOUD-10 on a device** — the status save with its log riding along goes to `CKDatabase` directly, outside the `CloudDatabase` seam: set a status, then another in the same minute, and check the history shows both once.
- **CLOUD-10's accepted edge** — non-atomic, a status attempt that conflicts still commits its log; a retry ending `superseded` leaves a history row for a status that never became current. Rare (an overlapping publish of our own).
- **Share check, closed link** — a link reopened from another of the owner's devices isn't seen for up to `closedShareCheckInterval` (a day), and Home's close-link prompt waits with it.
- **STORE-5 step 3** — let `mutate` derive widget reloads from a "what widgets draw" projection and delete the `reloadWidgets:` parameter (36 call sites decide by hand). Guard with a test walking `WidgetContent`'s inputs.
- **STORE-6 step 3** — the index is one 1.1 MB blob rewritten whole per moment; split hot (~200 newest, what extensions need) from cold.

1. **Re-entrancy gates (CLOUD-12, APP-9, rest).** `RunGate` now runs the sync and recovery passes; `Outbox`'s nine `isRetrying…`/`isRepublishing…` flags and `AppModel`'s `isBusy` still hand-roll it. Move them onto `RunGate`; `isBusy` → `pairingOperation: PairingOperation?`; one `RetryPacing` for throttle, storage-full, status backoff and rejected uploads.

3. **Archive as a stream (redesign A, APP-1's rest).** Chunks of ~25, batch-fetched with `desiredKeys` = the one asset straight into staging, failures classified (transient / gone / unsupported), `gone` listed for explicit confirmation.
4. **View decisions out of views (UI-6).** `HomeView.activeNotice`, `SyncFooter`'s precedence, `SettingsView.saveMemories/end` (the destructive gate), gallery `settle`, the status draft rules, `consumePendingRoute` → pure types in `Shared` with table tests.
5. **Duplicate UI (UI-7).** Four report dialogs, two identical caption fields, two Save-to-Photos routines, three hand-styled capsules.
