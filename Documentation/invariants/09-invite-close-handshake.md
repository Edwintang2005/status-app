# Invariant 9: Invite close is a two-step handshake, manual-only

Read before touching `CloudSync+Pairing` (`lockPairing`, `closeUnusedInvite`, `reopenInvite`), `SharePosture`, Settings' invite rows.

**Invite close is a two-step handshake, manual-only.** No single `CKShare` save makes a link-joined (public) partner private (a `role` flip is ignored; close + `addParticipant` drops the add — verified live). `lockPairing` closes (sweeping the public joiner), then re-adds them by `userRecordID` via `CKFetchShareParticipantsOperation` (no `lookupInfo` since iOS 17) as an *invited pending* private participant, confirmed when they tap the link once (`receiveInvite` same-zone → `reacceptShare`).
- Pending, the partner has no access, so it runs only on the owner's confirmed ask — Settings' "Close the invite link" (`AppModel.confirmingInviteReseat` → `closeInviteReseatingPartner`) or Diagnostics — never passively (auto-close in refresh stays disabled). Prompts only lead there: Home's "\<partner\>'s in" card (`showsCloseLinkPrompt`, once the server said the link is open this launch) and the hourly member count (`shareMemberCheckInterval`, "someone else has joined"; once the link is closed nobody new can join, so daily, `closedShareCheckInterval`, and a look from an earlier launch counts — `SharedStore.shareCheckedAt`).
- A failure after the close goes through `restoreAfterFailedClose`: `couldNotSecureShare` (reopened) or `inviteLeftClosed` (partner locked out until Settings' confirmed "Reopen the invite link"). `lockIfPartnerOnShare` never reports a closed, empty share as done. With nobody on the share, the close is `closeUnusedInvite`, refusing with `SyncError.inviteInUse` once anyone is on it — it can't evict. `lockPairing` refuses with `SyncError.tooManyOnShare` beyond one person besides the owner (it would re-seat a stranger along with the partner).
- Closes, reopens and the DEBUG sweep (`AppModel.sweepPublicJoiners`) run under `isChangingInviteLink` and refuse while another runs; a posture check overlapping one never overwrites it.
- Owner share calls go through `ownerPairing()`/`requireOwnerPairing()` (owner role, account available, the pairing's account — `isPairingAccount`); share saves confirm via `savedShare`. Demo mode shows neither prompt nor warning. No copy may say the link closes itself (`SettingsView.inviteFooter`, `InviteLinkSheet`).
