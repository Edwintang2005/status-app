# Invariant 20: Moderation is local and sticky (App Review 1.2)

Read before touching `Moderation.swift`, `ContentFilter`, `TermsView`, `AppModel.block`/`report`, any surface showing partner text.

**Moderation is local and sticky (App Review 1.2).** `TermsView` gates everything until `SharedStore.acceptedTermsVersion` reaches `AppConfig.termsVersion`.
- A reported moment goes into `hiddenMomentIDs`, filtered by `CloudSync.apply` from every delta (resyncs re-deliver it); a reported status hides by `hiddenPartnerStatusAt`. `AppModel.block` records the partner in `blockedOwnerRecordNames` *before* clearing the pairing and wipes even if the cloud unpair fails (the one place local-first is right); `acceptShare`/`discoverExistingPairing` refuse them.
- `ContentFilter` (word-level, partner text only, every surface; names skip `nameExemptions`, words that are also ordinary names) applies *only through `Moderation.swift`'s helpers*: `StatusPayload.moderation(reportedAt:revealed:…)` → `ModeratedStatus` (reported → no words or emoji; filtered → placeholder unless revealed) and `moderated(reportedAt:)`; `StatusHistoryEntry.moderation`/`moderated`; `Moment.moderatedCaption(revealed:)` → `ModeratedText` (flags a placeholder), `displayCaption(filterEnabled:)`, `displaySenderName(fallback:filterEnabled:)`; `Snapshot.moderatedPartnerName`/`partnerName(filterEnabled:)`. Names are user text too. Each helper takes the switch from its caller, read once per surface or pass (`SharedStore.contentFilterEnabled` is a file read): a library, an archive, a banner decision — never once per tile, caption or name; `PushBannerPolicy.decide` and `MomentBanner.content` read it once by default. An unopened reveal celebration is sealed the same way: pass `sealed: snapshot.partnerStatusSealed` (`ModeratedStatus.sealed`; history rows `sealedAt:`), so its words reach no surface but `CelebrationOverlay`. The widget (`WidgetContent`) and NSE read `hiddenPartnerStatusAt` like Home; `StatusPayload.wordsShown` is the receipt-side twin.
- Reports are `mailto:` `AppConfig.supportEmail` (24-hour response promised in the terms and on the site). Report/reveal are also `accessibilityActions` (partner card, library tiles); the celebration overlay has its own Report….
